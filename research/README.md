# Research workspace

Research material is separated from finished teaching documents so that exploratory
notes cannot silently become published conclusions.

- `evidence-ledger/`: normalized source records and claim-to-evidence mappings.
- `version-tracking/`: upstream snapshots, diffs and stale-document reports.
- `benchmarks/`: task manifests, metrics, failure taxonomy and reproducible results.
- `runtime-evidence/`: sanitized command records, test reports, traces and artifact hashes.
- `working-notes/`: incomplete hypotheses and investigation notes.

Private or credential-bearing evidence stays in ignored `private/` directories.
